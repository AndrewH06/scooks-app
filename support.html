Common questions

A Shelf Scan missed books, or got the titles wrong

Shelf Scan reads the spines from your photo, so it struggles with dim light, steep angles, and worn or stylised lettering. Shoot the shelf straight on, fill the frame with one or two rows, and scan again. Anything it still misses can be added by searching for the title.

Scanning stopped working for a while

Shelf Scan runs against a shared quota, so heavy use in a short window is throttled. The app tells you how long to wait. Scanning resumes on its own after that.

The camera screen is blank

Scooks needs camera access to photograph a shelf. Open the Settings app, find Scooks in the list, and turn Camera on.

A book has the wrong cover, or none at all

Covers come from the Hardcover catalogue, and some editions have none on file. Scooks draws a placeholder cover in that case, using colours from the book itself.

Can I move my collection to a new phone?

Not yet. Your collection is stored only on your device, which is what keeps it private, and there is no account or sync. Deleting the app erases it, so hold onto it until transfer arrives.

How do I request a feature or report a bug?

Email the address above. Concrete reports, especially with a screenshot, get fixed fastest.
