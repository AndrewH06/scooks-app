Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 September 2026

Scooks helps you catalogue the books you own and the ones you still want to read. This policy explains what the app handles, where it goes, and how long it stays.

We do not sell your data. We do not use it for advertising. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

What stays on your device

Your collection, your wishlist, your ratings, and the cover art Scooks downloads are all stored locally on your iPhone. None of it is uploaded to us, and we cannot see it.

Deleting the app removes that data permanently. We have no copy, so we cannot restore it.

Shelf Scan photos

When you photograph a bookshelf for a Shelf Scan, the photo is sent to our server and passed to Google's Gemini API, which reads the titles off the spines. Those titles are then looked up in the Hardcover book catalogue.

The photo is used only to produce that one result. It is never written to a database, a disk, or a backup on our side, and it is discarded as soon as the scan finishes. Google's handling of the image is governed by the Gemini API terms, under which submissions are not used to train their models.

Scooks never asks for access to your photo library. It uses the camera only, and only while you are on the Shelf Scan screen.

Book searches

When you search for a book or ask for similar titles, the search text or book identifier is sent to our server and passed to the Hardcover catalogue. We keep no record of what you searched for.

Anti-abuse data

To stop other people running up our API bills, the app sends two things with each scan request.

  • An installation identifier, a random value generated the first time you open the app. It is not your Apple ID, your device identifier, or anything that names you. It exists only to count requests per installation.
  • An Apple App Attest assertion, which proves the request came from a genuine copy of Scooks rather than a script.

Our server keeps the App Attest key record and a short-lived request counter tied to that identifier. Nothing in either describes you, your books, or your reading.

Children

Scooks is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Because your collection never leaves your phone, you control it directly. Deleting a book removes it. Deleting the app removes everything. If you want the anti-abuse records for your installation erased, write to us and we will remove them.

Changes to this policy

If anything here changes materially, we will update the date at the top of this page and note the change in the app's release notes.
