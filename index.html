Scooks

A book catalogue for iPhone

Take a photo of a bookshelf. Scooks reads the spines, finds each book, and adds them to a library that stays on your phone.

Features

  • Shelf Scan. One photo of a shelf becomes a list of books. You review the matches before anything is saved.
  • Collection and wishlist. Keep track of the books you own and the ones you want.
  • Ratings. Rate books with stars, and your top-rated books are gathered together.
  • Covers. Scooks uses real cover art when the catalogue has it and draws a cover when it doesn't.
  • Recommendations. Scooks suggests books similar to ones you liked.
  • No account. There's no sign-up, and your library is stored only on your iPhone.
