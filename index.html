Scooks

Your shelves, read in one photograph.

[sku̇ks] · for iPhone

Point the camera at a bookshelf. Scooks reads the spines, finds each title, and files them into a library you actually own, on a phone that keeps it to itself.

What it does

Shelf Scan

One photograph of a shelf becomes a list of books. Scooks reads the spines, matches them against a catalogue of millions of editions, and shows you what it found before anything is saved.

Owned and wanted, side by side

Your collection holds the books on your shelves. The wishlist holds the ones you are still hunting for. Books move between them as your reading does.

Rate what you have read

Give a book stars, or hold a cover to mark it a favourite. Your highest-rated books surface on their own.

Covers for everything

Real cover art wherever a catalogue has it. Where none exists, Scooks draws one from the book's own title and colours, so no shelf has a blank spine on it.

Something like this one

Open a book you loved and Scooks suggests titles close to it, drawn from what readers of that book read next.

No account, ever

There is no sign-up and no profile. Your library is written to your iPhone and nowhere else, and nobody, including us, can read it.
